# Photorealistic envelope materials

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool. These are generated photorealistic assets, not externally sourced photographs. The original PNGs are preserved locally in `.asset-tools/stationery-originals/`. Web copies are encoded as WebP, with the wax cutout's transparency preserved and checked.

| Saved project asset | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `public/images/stationery/envelope-landscape.webp` | Landscape envelope surface, 1600 × 900 |
| `public/images/stationery/envelope-portrait.webp` | Portrait envelope surface, 900 × 1600 |
| `public/images/stationery/wax-seal-my-cursive.webp` | Active transparent burgundy wax seal with raised champagne-gold cursive M & Y, 512 × 512 |
| `public/images/stationery/wax-seal-my.webp` | Earlier recessed serif seal, retained as the source for the cursive edit |

The responsive styles in `src/styles/envelope.css` align the seal to the photographed flap tips. The visible initials are part of the wax image; changing them requires replacing that asset. The actual control remains a keyboard-accessible button.

The cursive replacement uses the built-in `image_gen` tool in edit mode with `wax-seal-my.webp` as the reference. The full-resolution PNG is saved locally at `.asset-tools/stationery-originals/wax-seal-my-cursive.png`; the active WebP is 81,394 bytes. Transparent corner alpha was verified as zero after encoding.

### Cursive embossed seal edit prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset type: photorealistic transparent wax-seal website cutout, viewed straight overhead.
Input image 1 is the edit target: keep its realistic deep burgundy red poured sealing wax, irregular raised outer rim, tiny natural wax bubbles, broad glossy highlights, thickness, circular silhouette, and upper-left soft studio lighting.
Primary request: replace the existing dark recessed serif M&Y with the exact text "M & Y" in large, clearly readable, elegant connected cursive calligraphy. Use graceful wedding calligraphy with recognizable capital M and Y and a clear ampersand; moderate thick strokes and restrained swashes, generous spacing. Make the lettering physically raised / embossed above the wax surface, with substantial 3D bevels and subtle contact shadows. Color the raised letter tops warm pale champagne gold, a light luminous contrast against dark burgundy wax; gently reflective gold foil highlights and darker gold beveled sides give convincing real material and depth. This must look like macro product photography of an actual foil embossed wax seal, not flat text, not vector art. Keep the entire readable monogram centered and contained within the inner circular well, taking about 68% of its width. Remove every trace of the old serif monogram. Do not add any other text or icons.
Composition: one complete seal centered, almost filling square image with a small transparent margin, no cropped edges.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha. No paper, no rectangular background, no checkerboard baked in. Preserve the burgundy material and silhouette closely.

Soft diffuse light from upper-left, realistic subtle contact shadow within transparency, no paper backing. The stamp impression contains ONLY the exact text 'M & Y', clearly readable, elegant traditional serif lettering, deeply impressed into the SAME burgundy wax so edges catch light and recessed grooves are darker. Text verbatim: M & Y. Do not add any other lettering, crest, symbols, hearts, wreaths or decorative illustration. Must look like real sealing wax photographed on a real invitation, NOT a glossy plastic UI button, NOT metal, NOT a vector icon, NOT a smooth gradient circle, NOT illustration or CGI.
